Getting Connected Upon Arrival

Securing mobile data is one of the first practical steps upon arriving in Nepal. Whether you choose a physical local SIM card at Tribhuvan International Airport or configure a travel e-SIM before departure, knowing how networks behave across different regions keeps your journey seamless.

Ncell vs Nepal Telecom (NTC)

Nepal has two primary telecommunications operators providing comprehensive 4G coverage across urban centers and major transit highways:

  • Nepal Telecom (NTC): State-owned carrier with extensive tower reach, often providing superior signals in remote mountain trekking zones (such as parts of the Everest and Annapurna regions).
  • Ncell: A private operator offering competitive tourist data packages, excellent speeds in Kathmandu and Pokhara, and easy activation booths right at the airport terminal.

Registration Rules & Documentation

Government regulations require official tourist registration for all local SIM cards purchased in Nepal.

  • Required Items: Bring your physical passport, a copy of your active Nepal tourist visa stamp, and a passport-sized photograph.
  • Authorized Outlets: Purchase directly from official carrier counters at the airport or authorized retail stores in Thamel or Lakeside Pokhara to ensure your SIM is properly activated under your name.

Coverage Follows Geography

Cellular connectivity in Nepal is heavily dictated by topography. While 4G data speeds are reliable across Kathmandu, Pokhara, and major paved highways, signals drop quickly inside deep river gorges, high mountain passes, and remote valleys where deep ridges block line-of-sight tower signals.

Keep Offline Essentials Ready

Because mobile data cannot be guaranteed in high-altitude gorges or during unexpected network maintenance, always maintain a robust offline backup:

  • Download offline regional maps (such as Maps.me or Gaia GPS).
  • Save digital copies of hotel bookings, flight tickets, and travel insurance details locally on your device.
  • Note down key emergency contact numbers and embassy details offline.
