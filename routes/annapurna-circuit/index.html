Route overview

A modern circuit plan that accounts for road sections, acclimatisation and a finish in Pokhara.

Modern road access has changed the classic circuit, making trailhead and walking-section choices central to the plan. A conservative itinerary protects acclimatisation before Thorong La and avoids racing to match transport.

Transportation

Bus or jeep to and from trailheads. Compare the full transfer—boarding point, luggage, stops and final arrival—not only the advertised travel time.

Last verified September 2026. Schedules and road conditions change; confirm directly before travel.

Major stops

  • Kathmandu — Finalise permits, guide arrangements and a realistic trailhead transfer.
  • Besisahar / Dharapani — Road construction changes where many trekkers begin walking; confirm locally.
  • Manang — Use a full acclimatisation day and do not ascend with worsening altitude symptoms.
  • Thorong La — Cross only with suitable weather, health and an early start; the pass is not a place to improvise.
  • Muktinath — A sacred Hindu and Buddhist site where respectful dress and behaviour matter.
  • Pokhara — Use the finish for recovery and retain a buffer before onward travel.

Where to stay & eat

Use teahouses along the walking route and expect simpler facilities above Manang. Dal bhat, soups, noodles and potatoes are widely available; carry treated water rather than relying on disposable bottles. Pokhara makes the natural recovery base.

Route alternatives

  • Start walking higher only if the itinerary still preserves acclimatisation.
  • Finish via Jomsom when lower-trail road sections do not suit the trip.
  • Add Tilicho Lake only with extra days, suitable conditions and local advice.

Safety & useful tips

  • Do not cross Thorong La in poor weather or while experiencing altitude symptoms.
  • Check road and trail conditions before fixing the walking start.
  • Carry insurance that covers the maximum planned altitude and evacuation.
  • Keep the route flexible enough to add a rest or acclimatisation day.
