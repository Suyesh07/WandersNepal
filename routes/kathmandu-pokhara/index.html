Route overview

Nepal’s classic intercity journey, made better by choosing the right transport—and better still by slowing down.

Choose the road for changing river-and-hill scenery or the flight for speed, while treating either arrival time as approximate. A two-night minimum in Pokhara makes the transfer worthwhile.

Transportation

Tourist bus, domestic flight or private vehicle. Compare the full transfer—boarding point, luggage, stops and final arrival—not only the advertised travel time.

Last verified September 2026. Schedules and road conditions change; confirm directly before travel.

Major stops

  • Kathmandu — Start rested, confirm the departure point and avoid a same-day international-flight connection.
  • Trishuli corridor — The road follows river valleys and hill sections; meal and comfort stops depend on the operator.
  • Pokhara — Use Lakeside for convenience, or stay farther from the waterfront for a quieter base.

Where to stay & eat

In Kathmandu, stay near the neighbourhoods you plan to explore rather than automatically choosing the busiest district. In Pokhara, Lakeside is easiest for transport and dining; the northern lake shore and nearby valleys are quieter. On the road, carry water and a simple snack because stops vary.

Route alternatives

  • Break the road journey in Bandipur for one or two nights.
  • Fly one direction and travel overland in the other to preserve time without missing the landscape.
  • Continue from Pokhara to Ghandruk only when at least two additional nights are available.

Safety & useful tips

  • Do not schedule the arrival against an international departure.
  • Use a seat belt where fitted and avoid unnecessary night driving.
  • Confirm the exact Kathmandu boarding point and Pokhara drop-off before departure.
  • Expect delays during monsoon, road works and holiday traffic.
