Route overview

Turn the transfer west into a journey with a hill-town pause and time for ridge walking.

Bandipur changes a standard westbound transfer into a journey. The route suits travelers who value walking-scale towns and want to arrive in Pokhara without treating the highway as dead time.

Day-by-day plan

Kathmandu to BandipurBus to Dumre, then local transfer uphill
BandipurBazaar lanes, viewpoints and an unhurried village evening
Bandipur countrysideOptional Ramkot walk or extra heritage time
Bandipur to PokharaDescend to Dumre and continue west
PokharaLakeside, Old Bazaar and a flexible ridge or lake day

Transportation

Tourist bus plus local jeep, or private vehicle. Compare the full transfer—boarding point, luggage, stops and final arrival—not only the advertised travel time.

Last verified September 2026. Schedules and road conditions change; confirm directly before travel.

Major stops

  • Kathmandu — Confirm whether your bus stops at Dumre and keep the Bandipur transfer details offline.
  • Dumre — This highway junction is the change point for the climb to Bandipur, not the destination itself.
  • Bandipur — Stay overnight to experience the bazaar after day visitors leave and to allow for cloud around viewpoints.
  • Pokhara — Arrive with enough daylight to settle in and keep the next morning flexible for mountain visibility.

Where to stay & eat

Stay inside or just beyond Bandipur’s pedestrian bazaar for an evening and early morning in the old town. In Pokhara, choose Lakeside for convenience or a quieter edge-of-lake area. Bandipur’s locally run guesthouses are part of the route’s appeal.

Route alternatives

  • Travel directly to Pokhara if road conditions consume the Bandipur night.
  • Add a second Bandipur night for the Ramkot walk or weather flexibility.
  • Continue from Pokhara to Ghandruk for a short village trek.

Safety & useful tips

  • Confirm the Dumre–Bandipur connection before boarding a through bus.
  • Aim to complete the uphill transfer before dark.
  • Use footwear with grip on steep stone lanes, especially in rain.
  • Keep the final Pokhara day flexible when connecting onward.
