Overview & Scope

At Wanders Nepal, we respect your privacy. This policy outlines what information we collect when you visit our website, subscribe to our newsletter, or submit custom trip planning inquiries, and how we protect that information.

We operate on a simple principle: we only collect the data necessary to send you monthly field notes or respond to your trip planning requests. We do not engage in aggressive behavioral profiling or sell user data to advertising brokers.

Information We Collect

We gather two primary categories of information:

1. Information You Provide Voluntarily

  • Newsletter Subscriptions: Your email address when you sign up for our monthly route dispatches.
  • Trip Inquiries: Details you enter into our custom trip planner or contact forms (such as your name, email, group size, duration preferences, and custom notes).

2. Automatically Collected Technical Data

Like most static websites, our hosting and deployment platforms automatically log standard technical telemetry when you browse our pages, including:

  • Browser type and operating system version.
  • Referring website URLs and pages visited on Wanders Nepal.
  • Timestamp and generic IP address routing data used strictly for site security and performance.

How We Use Your Data

The information we collect is utilized exclusively for the following operational purposes:

  • Delivering Field Notes: Sending monthly itineraries, seasonal weather updates, and slow-travel guides to active newsletter subscribers.
  • Custom Trip Coordination: Responding to your bespoke route inquiries with personalized itineraries and logistics advice.
  • Site Integrity & Security: Protecting our hosting infrastructure from malicious bot traffic, spam submissions, and unauthorized server breaches.

Forms & Third-Party Hosting Handlers

Our forms (such as newsletter signups and trip planner requests) are processed via secure serverless form handlers (such as Netlify forms). These services utilize built-in spam protection honeypots (`bot-field`) to prevent automated script spam. Data submitted through these forms is stored securely on encrypted server infrastructure solely for processing your request.

Cookies & Analytics

Wanders Nepal does not use intrusive tracking cookies or third-party behavioral ad pixels. We may use lightweight, privacy-focused analytics to track overall aggregate site traffic (e.g., which itineraries are most popular) so we know which guides to update. You can configure your browser to block all cookies if you prefer absolute anonymity while browsing.

Your Data Rights & Control

You maintain full control over your personal data at all times:

  • Unsubscribe Anytime: Every newsletter email includes a clear, one-click unsubscribe link at the bottom. Once clicked, your email is permanently removed from our active dispatch list.
  • Data Deletion Requests: If you wish to delete any custom trip inquiries or personal records submitted through our site, contact us directly, and we will purge your data promptly.

Contact & Policy Updates

If you have any questions regarding this privacy policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to our editorial and operations desk through our Contact Page. Any future updates to this policy will be posted directly on this page with a revised effective date.
