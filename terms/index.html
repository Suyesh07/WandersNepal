Overview & Scope

Welcome to Wanders Nepal. By accessing our website, reading our route guides, or utilizing our trip planning tools, you agree to comply with and be bound by the following terms and conditions. Please read them carefully before planning your journey or setting foot on trail networks.

Information, Not a Booking Agency

Wanders Nepal functions exclusively as an independent digital travel-planning and routing resource. We do not operate transport fleets, issue official government permits, sell commercial travel insurance, or book direct teahouse accommodations.

Our field notes, elevation charts, and budget breakdowns are provided for general guidance and educational planning. They do not replace professional medical advice, certified mountain guide expertise, or official agency bookings.

Conditions, Weather & Rules Change

Mountain environments, high-altitude weather windows, road conditions, permit fees, and official government regulations in Nepal change frequently and often without prior notice.

While our editorial desk verifies facts regularly, you remain solely responsible for reconfirming critical logistical details—such as STOL flight operations, park permit costs, and trail access restrictions—with official authorities (e.g., Nepal Tourism Board or Department of Immigration) before acting on any guide.

Travel Decisions & Personal Responsibility

Himalayan trekking and remote travel involve inherent physical risks, including Acute Mountain Sickness (AMS), severe weather shifts, and difficult evacuation logistics.

  • Personal Suitability: You are entirely responsible for assessing your physical fitness, medical preparedness, and technical ability for any route.
  • Permissions & Insurance: You must secure all necessary permits, TIMS cards, restricted area paperwork, and comprehensive medical insurance covering high-altitude helicopter evacuation.
  • Conservative Choices: You retain full liability for making safe, conservative decisions when faced with changing weather, fatigue, or altitude symptoms.

Intellectual Property & Content Usage

All original text, elevation profile SVGs, layout designs, and graphics published on Wanders Nepal are protected under copyright law. You may view, download, or print individual guide pages for your personal, non-commercial travel planning. Commercial republication, scraping, or systematic redistribution of our route data without prior written consent is strictly prohibited.
