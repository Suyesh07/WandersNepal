5 Days, Balanced and Unhurried

With five days on the ground, you have the breathing room to combine Nepal’s cultural heartland with its premier alpine leisure valley. The critical logistical rule for a 5-day window: fly between Kathmandu and Pokhara. The 25-minute domestic flight saves 14 to 18 hours of highway transit, giving you two full days of mountain reflections, wooden boat paddling, and ridge viewpoints.

Day 1: Kathmandu Arrival, Sacred Patan & Evening Aarti

Day 01

Morning to Midday: Arrival & Patan Heritage. Land at Tribhuvan International Airport (TIA). Head directly to your base in Patan (Lalitpur). Walk through Patan Durbar Square (entry: NPR 1,000), taking in the 17th-century stone Krishna Mandir, the Patan Museum in Keshav Narayan Chowk, and the tranquil courtyard of the Golden Temple (Kwa Bahal).

Late Afternoon: Pashupatinath Sandhya Aarti. Transfer northeast to Pashupatinath Temple (entry: NPR 1,000). Walk the stone terraces across the sacred Bagmati River, observing evening rituals and the 6:00 PM fire-lit Sandhya Aarti ceremony featuring conch horns, brass oil lamps, and Vedic chanting.

Overnight: Restored Newar boutique guesthouse in Patan.

Day 2: Morning Boudhanath, Flight to Pokhara & Lakefront Sunset

Day 02

Early Morning: Boudhanath Stupa. Arrive by 6:30 AM at Boudhanath (entry: NPR 400). Join Buddhist pilgrims for morning circumambulations (kora) around the giant whitewashed dome. Enjoy a rooftop breakfast with views of spinning prayer wheels and drifting juniper smoke.

Midday: Flight to Pokhara. Transfer to TIA domestic terminal for the 25-minute flight to Pokhara Airport (PKR). Sit on the right side of the aircraft for views of the Langtang, Manaslu, and Annapurna massifs.

Afternoon & Dusk: Phewa Tal & Shanti Stupa. Check into your Lakeside hotel. Rent a wooden boat (doonga) to cross Phewa Tal, then hike 45 minutes up through Raniban forest to the gleaming white Shanti Stupa (World Peace Pagoda) for sunset over the lake and the fluted spire of Machhapuchhare.

Overnight: Lakeside, Pokhara.

Day 3: Sarangkot Sunrise, Mountain Museum & Thakali Feast

Day 03

Dawn: Sarangkot Himalayan Panorama (1,600m). Take an early 30-minute drive or cable car to Sarangkot ridge before first light. Watch the dawn alpenglow sweep across Dhaulagiri (8,167m), Annapurna I (8,091m), and Machhapuchhare (6,993m).

Mid-Morning (Optional): Tandem Paragliding. Launch from Sarangkot or Toripani with an accredited pilot, soaring on mountain thermals over green terraces before landing near the lake.

Afternoon: International Mountain Museum & Old Bazaar. Tour the International Mountain Museum to explore mountaineering expeditions, flora, fauna, and Sherpa heritage. Later, explore the 18th-century Newar brick architecture and Bindhyabasini Temple in Pokhara's Old Town.

Dinner: Authentic Thakali Dal Bhat dinner in Central Lakeside. Overnight: Lakeside, Pokhara.

Day 4: Peaceful Begnas Lake & Return Flight to Kathmandu

Day 04

Morning: Begnas Tal Escape. Drive 30 minutes east of Pokhara to tranquil Begnas Tal. Walk peaceful shoreline trails, visit hillside organic coffee smallholders, and enjoy a quiet lakeside lunch away from tourist bustle.

Afternoon: Return Flight to Kathmandu. Transfer to Pokhara Airport for your afternoon flight back to Kathmandu. Sit on the left side of the aircraft for mountain panoramas.

Evening: Thamel or Bhaktapur Twilight. Check into your hotel. Spend a relaxed evening exploring the artisanal craft alleys of Thamel or transfer east to Bhaktapur for a quiet heritage dinner.

Overnight: Kathmandu or Bhaktapur.

Day 5: Medieval Bhaktapur Durbar Square & International Departure

Day 05

Morning: Bhaktapur Durbar Square. Spend your final morning in the preserved medieval citadel of Bhaktapur (entry: NPR 1,800). Admire the five-tiered Nyatapola Temple, walk through Pottery Square to see artisans working on open-air wheels, and savor a fresh bowl of sweet Juju Dhau ("King Curd").

Midday: Final Shopping & Airport Transfer. Pick up authentic souvenirs—organic loose-leaf orthodox tea, hand-loomed Dhaka scarves, or singing bowls. Transfer smoothly to Tribhuvan International Airport (TIA) for your onward flight.

Departure: Airport check-in 3 hours before international flight departure.

Dining & Regional Flavors

This 5-day loop introduces two distinct culinary traditions:

  • Newar Cuisine in Patan: Order a Samay Baji platter featuring beaten rice (baji), spicy fire-roasted water buffalo (Chhoyela), black lentil patties (Wo / Bara), and roasted soybeans.
  • Thakali Khana in Pokhara: A traditional brass thali of steamed rice, slow-cooked black lentils tempered with wild mountain herb (jimbu), spicy dry-roasted potatoes, wild greens, and radish-timur pickle.
  • Fresh Lake Fish in Begnas: Locally caught freshwater carp or trout, spiced with turmeric, chili, and garlic, and pan-fried at shoreline family kitchens.
  • Bhaktapur Juju Dhau: Thick, rich buffalo-milk yogurt naturally sweetened and fermented in unglazed clay pots.

Estimated Costs & Budget Breakdown

A realistic breakdown for 5 days covering domestic flights, boutique lodging, monument fees, and meals:

Budget Backpacker NPR 5,500–8,500 / Day (~$42–$65)

Clean family guesthouses, local diner meals, public airport taxis, and 1 domestic flight (returning via tourist bus).

Mid-Range Comfort (Recommended) NPR 14,000–22,000 / Day (~$105–$165)

Boutique heritage hotels in Patan and lakeside resorts in Pokhara, round-trip domestic flights, private cars for sightseeing, and all site entries.

Premium Heritage & Leisure NPR 35,000+ / Day (~$260+)

Five-star heritage suites (e.g., Dwarika's or Pavilions Himalayas), dedicated private chauffeur throughout, private boat charters, and guided cultural tours.

Alternative 5-Day Blueprints

If your focus leans toward active hiking or wildlife safaris rather than city culture, consider these alternative 5-day routes:

Alternative 01 Ghorepani Poon Hill Mini-Trek

Fly to Pokhara on Day 1; drive to Birethanti and trek up through rhododendron forests to Ulleri and Ghorepani; catch sunrise over Dhaulagiri from Poon Hill (3,210m) on Day 3; descend via Ghandruk Gurung village; return to Kathmandu on Day 5.

Alternative 02 Kathmandu Valley to Chitwan Jungle Safari

Spend Day 1 in Kathmandu; fly or drive south to Chitwan National Park on Day 2; spend Days 2–4 on river safaris, jeep tracks, and Tharu village walks tracking one-horned rhinos; fly back to Kathmandu on Day 5.

Planning Rules for a 5-Day Trip

  • Book Morning Flights (Before 10:00 AM): Early flights between Kathmandu and Pokhara are less prone to afternoon haze and thermal wind delays.
  • Carry Physical Cash for Monuments: Ticket counters at Bhaktapur, Patan, and Pashupatinath require cash in Nepali Rupees. Keep small denominations handy.
  • Choose the Right Aircraft Seat: On the outbound flight from Kathmandu to Pokhara, sit on the right side for views of the Himalayan chain. On the return flight to Kathmandu, sit on the left side.
  • Keep Night 4 Near the Airport: Staying in Kathmandu or Bhaktapur on your final night avoids transport stress on your departure day.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I drive between Kathmandu and Pokhara instead of flying?

Driving the 200 km Prithvi Highway currently takes 7 to 9 hours each way due to road-widening work. On a 5-day itinerary, driving consumes two entire days in transit. Flying takes just 25 minutes and protects your daylight hours for sightseeing.

Do I need trekking permits for this 5-day itinerary?

No. Visiting Kathmandu, Patan, Bhaktapur, Pokhara, Sarangkot, and Begnas Lake requires zero trekking permits or TIMS cards. You only pay standard local heritage ticket fees at monument gates.

What should I pack for 5 days in Kathmandu and Pokhara?

Pack comfortable walking shoes for stone steps and temple courtyards, modest clothing that covers shoulders and knees, a light fleece or jacket for early mornings on Sarangkot, sunglasses, sunscreen, and a universal power adapter.
