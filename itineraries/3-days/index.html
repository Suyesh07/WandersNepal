72 Hours in Nepal: The Complete Day-by-Day Masterplan

With only three days in Nepal, attempting cross-country road transfers to Pokhara or Chitwan results in spending more than 16 hours stuck on expanding arterial highways. This curated route clusters stops geographically within the Kathmandu bowl and its eastern rim, ensuring you maximize time in living courtyards, ancient monasteries, and pine-clad viewpoints.

Day 1: Royal Patan Courtyards, Metalworking Alleys & Sacred Cremations

Day 01

Morning: Arrival & Base Check-in (Patan / Lalitpur). Touch down at Tribhuvan International Airport (TIA). Clear immigration, secure a local NTC or Ncell tourist e-SIM, and take a direct 20-minute taxi south across the Bagmati River to Patan. Settle into a restored Newar heritage guesthouse around Switha Tole or Mangal Bazaar rather than noisy Thamel.

Midday: Patan Durbar Square & Hidden Bahals. Step directly into Patan Durbar Square (entry: NPR 1,000 for foreign nationals). Tour the Keshav Narayan Chowk housing the world-class Patan Museum, and admire the stone masterwork of the 17th-century Krishna Mandir. Slip into the narrow alleys to find the Golden Temple (Kwa Bahal), a 12th-century Buddhist monastery, and observe master metalsmiths practicing lost-wax bronze casting in the surrounding foundries.

Afternoon & Dusk: Pashupatinath Sandhya Aarti. At 4:30 PM, drive 25 minutes northeast to the sacred Hindu precinct of Pashupatinath (entry: NPR 1,000). Walk the eastern bank of the holy Bagmati River across from the royal cremation pyres at Arya Ghat. At 6:00 PM, witness the rhythmic, fire-lit ceremony of the Sandhya Aarti, accompanied by brass oil lamps, Vedic chants, drums, and ringing bells.

Overnight: Restored Newar boutique guesthouse in Patan.

Day 2: Dawn Monastic Kora, Swayambhunath Ridge & Valley Rim Ascent

Day 02

Dawn: Morning Kora at Boudhanath Stupa. Arrive by 6:30 AM at Boudhanath (entry: NPR 400), the spiritual heart of Tibetan Buddhism in Nepal. Join Tibetan pilgrims, monks, and locals as they circumambulate the colossal whitewashed stupa clockwise, spinning brass prayer wheels through drifting clouds of burning juniper incense. Head to an elevated rooftop terrace for organic coffee and fresh Tibetan flatbread.

Midday: Swayambhunath (The Monkey Temple). Drive west across town to the forested hill of Swayambhunath (entry: NPR 200). Climb the ancient 365 stone steps flanked by painted stone chaityas and macaques to view the valley floor from above, where Buddhist prayer flags weave directly into Tantric Hindu shrines.

Afternoon: Drive East to the Nagarkot Ridge (2,175m). Meet your private driver and head east along the Araniko Highway past Bhaktapur, climbing the pine-scented switchbacks to Nagarkot (municipal fee: NPR 340). Check into a mountain-facing lodge.

Sunset: Watch the evening light turn the snowfields of Ganesh Himal, Langtang Lirung, and Jugal Himal to shades of amber and pink directly from your terrace. Overnight: Nagarkot ridge resort.

Day 3: Himalayan Sunrise, Medieval Bhaktapur & Airport Exit

Day 03

Dawn: Nagarkot Sunrise. Awaken at 5:30 AM to catch the alpenglow illuminating the 300-kilometer Himalayan crest. On crystal-clear autumn and winter mornings, scan the far eastern horizon for the optical point of Mount Everest.

Morning: Descent to Bhaktapur Durbar Square. Enjoy a relaxed outdoor breakfast, then drive 45 minutes down the hill into Bhaktapur (entry: NPR 1,800), the best-preserved medieval city-state in Nepal. Enter through the grand stone gates into a car-free heritage zone of reddish brick, timber carvings, and stone temples.

Midday: Pottery Squares & Five-Story Pagodas. Wander past the soaring five-tiered roof of Nyatapola Temple in Taumadhi Square—the tallest pagoda in Nepal. Visit the 55-Window Palace, watch potters spin clay vessels in sunlit Pottery Square, and pause at a street stall for a traditional clay bowl of Juju Dhau ("King Curd"), Bhaktapur's famed thick, sweet yogurt.

Afternoon: Direct Airport Departure. Bhaktapur lies just 12 kilometers east of Tribhuvan International Airport (TIA). A 30-to-40-minute drive delivers you straight to the departure terminal, completely bypassing central Kathmandu's notorious afternoon bottlenecks.

Route Logic: Spatial Clustering & Elevation Profile

The primary failure of most short visits is zigzagging across the city during peak traffic hours (9:00–11:00 AM and 4:30–6:30 PM). This itinerary flows systematically: South (Patan) → Northeast (Pashupati & Boudha) → East Ridge (Nagarkot) → East Valley (Bhaktapur) → Airport.

Where to Eat: 72 Hours of Authentic Gastronomy

Rather than dining inside international hotels, sample authentic culinary lineages at each stop:

  • Patan Heritage Lunch: Order a complete Samay Baji platter at a traditional Newari kitchen in Mangal Bazaar—beaten rice (baji), spicy fire-roasted spiced buffalo (Chhoyela), savory black lentil patties (Wo / Bara), roasted soybeans, and dry-roasted potato curry.
  • Boudhanath Tibetan Breakfast: Enjoy freshly steamed vegetable or chicken Tibetan momos, hand-pulled noodle soup (Thukpa), and salted butter tea (Su-chya) overlooking the whitewashed stupa.
  • Bhaktapur Clay-Pot Dessert: Sample fresh, naturally sweetened Juju Dhau ("King Curd") served in disposable clay pots in Taumadhi Square.
  • Nagarkot Evening Hearth: Savor a steaming bowl of wild mushroom soup and authentic Nepali Dal Bhat Tarkari featuring locally grown organic hill potatoes and wild mustard greens (saag).

Premium Heritage NPR 26,000+ / Day (~$195+)

Alternative 3-Day Blueprints

If your travel goals focus on mountain trekking or lake adventure rather than temple courtyards, switch to one of these verified alternative sequences:

Alternative 01 The Pokhara Flight Escape

Fly 25 minutes from Kathmandu to Pokhara. Spend Day 1 boating on Phewa Tal and hiking to Shanti Stupa; Day 2 catching sunrise from Sarangkot and tandem paragliding; Day 3 exploring the Old Bazaar and Begnas Lake before flying back to Kathmandu.

Alternative 02 The Shivapuri Rim Ridge Hike

Start at Sundarijal and trek through the oak canopies of Shivapuri Nagarjun National Park to Chisapani (2,300m) on Day 1. Hike the scenic northern ridge to Nagarkot on Day 2; descend past Newar farms to ancient Changu Narayan on Day 3.

Essential Planning Rules for a 72-Hour Stay

  • Hire a Dedicated Vehicle & Driver: Do not rely on hailing street taxis point-to-point. Pre-booking an air-conditioned sedan or Scorpio for the entire 3 days (approx. NPR 12,000–16,000 total) saves hours of bargaining and waiting.
  • Carry Cash in Small Denominations: Heritage ticket booths, village tea stalls, and local artisans rarely accept credit cards. Withdraw NPR 10,000 to 15,000 in cash at airport ATMs upon arrival.
  • Pack Layered Clothing: Kathmandu Valley temperatures are mild during the day (20–25°C in spring and autumn), but Nagarkot sits at 2,175 meters, where night and dawn temperatures routinely drop to 6–10°C. Bring a fleece or light down jacket.
  • Keep the Final Night East of Kathmandu: Staying in Nagarkot or Bhaktapur on Night 2 places you within 30 to 45 minutes of the airport without having to cross downtown Kathmandu's congested river bridges.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is 3 days really enough time to visit Nepal?

While 3 days is too short for high-altitude base camp trekking or multi-day wildlife safaris, it is the perfect window to experience the UNESCO World Heritage monuments, Newar culinary traditions, and Himalayan sunrise viewpoints of the Kathmandu Valley without feeling rushed.

Can I go to Pokhara on a 3-day trip?

Only if you travel strictly by air (25-minute domestic flights each way). Driving the 200 km Prithvi Highway takes 7 to 9 hours each direction due to highway widening work, consuming two full days in road transit.

How do I handle the airport transfer on Day 1?

Pre-arrange an airport pickup through your Patan guesthouse, or use the official prepaid taxi counter inside the international arrival hall (fixed fares: approx. NPR 800–1,000 to Patan).
