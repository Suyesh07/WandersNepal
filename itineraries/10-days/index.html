10 Days in Nepal: The Complete Day-by-Day Masterplan

Ten days is the sweet spot for travelers who want to venture into the Himalayas without undertaking an arduous multi-week high-pass expedition. This itinerary combines domestic aviation with 4WD mountain roads to access Lower Mustang—a dramatic trans-Himalayan desert region north of the Annapurna range—before returning via the preserved 18th-century merchant hill town of Bandipur.

Day 1: Arrival in Kathmandu & Medieval Patan Courtyards

Day 01

Morning to Midday: Arrival & Check-In. Land at Tribhuvan International Airport (TIA). Meet your driver and transfer south to Patan (Lalitpur). Check into a restored Newar heritage lodge around Mangal Bazaar.

Afternoon: Patan Durbar Square & Hidden Bahals. Explore Patan Durbar Square (entry: NPR 1,000). Visit the Patan Museum in Keshav Narayan Chowk, admire the stone carvings of Krishna Mandir, and walk the quiet courtyards of the Golden Temple (Kwa Bahal) to observe traditional bronze metal casting in Taksar alleys.

Overnight: Boutique heritage guesthouse in Patan.

Day 2: Morning Boudhanath Kora & Domestic Flight to Pokhara

Day 02

Early Morning: Boudhanath Stupa (6:30 AM). Join Tibetan pilgrims for morning circumambulations (kora) around Boudhanath (entry: NPR 400). Savor breakfast on an elevated terrace overlooking the prayer wheels and burning juniper incense.

Midday: Flight to Pokhara (822m). Board a 25-minute scheduled flight from Kathmandu to Pokhara Airport (PKR), sitting on the right side for Himalayan panoramas.

Afternoon & Sunset: Phewa Tal & Peace Pagoda. Check into your Lakeside hotel. Rent a wooden boat (doonga) to row across Phewa Tal, then hike up through the Raniban forest to Shanti Stupa (World Peace Pagoda) for views of Machhapuchhare (6,993m) reflected on the lake.

Overnight: Lakeside, Pokhara.

Day 3: Mountain Flight to Jomsom & Medieval Kagbeni Fort

Day 03

Early Morning: Flight through the Kali Gandaki Gorge. Board an early STOL flight (approx. 20 minutes) from Pokhara to Jomsom Airport (2,720m), flying directly between the Annapurna and Dhaulagiri massifs before the gale-force afternoon winds rise.

Mid-Morning: Overland to Kagbeni (2,810m). Take a 45-minute shared or private jeep up the wide gravel riverbed of the Kali Gandaki to the fortified oasis village of Kagbeni.

Afternoon: Exploring the Gateway of Lo. Walk through narrow, covered mud-brick tunnels, inspect ancient animist spirit-traps (kheni), and visit the 15th-century Kag Chode Thupten Sampheling Gompa overlooking the river confluence. Search the gravel flats for fossilized ammonites (Shaligram).

Overnight: Traditional Tibetan-style stone lodge in Kagbeni.

Day 4: Ascent to Muktinath Sanctuary & 108 Sacred Spouts

Day 04

Morning: Hike or 4WD to Muktinath (3,710m). Ascend the switchbacks through terraced barley fields to the sacred pilgrimage complex of Muktinath (Chumig Gyatsa). The drive takes 45 minutes, or you can hike 3.5 hours along the scenic trail via Jharkot.

Midday: Temple Courtyards & Jwala Mai. Walk beneath the 108 freezing brass cow-headed water spouts (Muktidhara), visit the central pagoda dedicated to Lord Vishnu, and step inside Dhola Mebar Gompa to witness the continuous natural gas flame burning over spring water at Jwala Mai.

Late Afternoon: Ranipauwa Exploration. Stroll the bazaar street of Ranipauwa, meeting sadhus, Buddhist pilgrims, and trekkers descending from the Thorong La pass.

Overnight: Mountain lodge in Ranipauwa / Muktinath.

Day 5: Downhill Walk to Jharkot Fort & Cobblestone Marpha

Day 05

Morning: Walk through Jharkot Fortress (3,550m). Take a peaceful 1-hour downhill walk to the ancient mud-brick village of Jharkot. Tour its red Sakya monastery and traditional amchi herbal medicine center.

Midday: Transfer to Marpha (2,670m). Take a private jeep south past Jomsom to the historic village of Marpha, the cultural heart of the Thakali people.

Afternoon: Apple Orchards & Flagstone Alleys. Wander Marpha’s spotless stone-paved streets, lined with flat-roofed, whitewashed homes and wood stacks. Visit a local distillery to sample organic apple brandy and freshly pressed cider.

Overnight: Traditional Thakali inn in Marpha.

Day 6: Canyon Descent & Tatopani Natural Hot Springs

Day 06

Morning: Down the Kali Gandaki Gorge. Board a 4WD Scorpio jeep heading south through the deepest gorge on earth. Transition from trans-Himalayan desert into subtropical pine and rhododendron forests past Ghasa and the waterfalls of Rupse Chhahara.

Afternoon: Soak in Tatopani Hot Springs (1,190m). Arrive in Tatopani ("hot water") by 1:00 PM. Check in and spend the afternoon soaking in natural riverside mineral thermal pools along the Kali Gandaki riverbanks.

Overnight: Garden lodge in Tatopani.

Day 7: Drive to Pokhara & Leisure Afternoon on the Lake

Day 07

Morning: Scenic Drive via Beni. Drive south from Tatopani along the paved highway through Beni and Nayapul, arriving back in Pokhara by midday (approx. 4 hours).

Afternoon: Rest & Recovery. Enjoy a slow afternoon in Lakeside: get a traditional deep-tissue trekking massage, browse gear shops, or relax in a garden cafe along Phewa Tal.

Overnight: Lakeside, Pokhara.

Day 8: Sarangkot Sunrise & Overland to Bandipur Citadel

Day 08

Dawn: Sunrise from Sarangkot (1,600m). Early morning drive up to Sarangkot ridge to watch the first golden rays strike Machhapuchhare, Annapurna South, and Dhaulagiri.

Midday: Drive East to Bandipur (1,030m). Depart Pokhara along the Prithvi Highway, turning up the winding ridge road at Dumre to reach the preserved 18th-century Newar trading citadel of Bandipur (approx. 2.5–3 hours).

Afternoon: Car-Free Cobblestone Promenade. Stroll through Bandipur's pedestrian main street, lined with wooden-balconied merchant houses and stone temples. Hike 20 minutes to Thani Mai Temple for 360-degree ridge sunset views.

Overnight: Restored boutique merchant mansion in Bandipur.

Day 9: Bandipur Ridge Dawn & Highway Return to Kathmandu

Day 09

Dawn: Ridge Alpenglow. Catch morning views of the Manaslu range (8,163m) and Ganesh Himal floating above the morning valley inversion fog.

Morning to Midday: Overland Return to Kathmandu. Depart Bandipur by private vehicle for the scenic 4.5-to-5.5-hour drive back along the Trishuli River canyon to the capital.

Evening: Pashupatinath Aarti. Head to Pashupatinath Temple to stand along the sacred riverbanks for the 6:00 PM Sandhya Aarti ceremony featuring oil lamps, temple bells, and chanting.

Overnight: Kathmandu or Patan.

Day 10: Medieval Bhaktapur Durbar Square & Airport Exit

Day 10

Morning: Living Museum of Bhaktapur. Spend your final morning in the medieval brick-and-timber city-state of Bhaktapur (entry: NPR 1,800). Admire the five-story Nyatapola Temple, walk through Pottery Square, and sample a fresh bowl of sweet Juju Dhau ("King Curd").

Afternoon: International Airport Transfer. Bhaktapur sits just 12 km east of Tribhuvan International Airport (TIA). A 35-minute drive brings you directly to the departure terminal without battling central city traffic.

Departure: Check in at TIA 3 hours before your international flight.

Regional Foodways: Valley, Desert & Mountain

This itinerary covers four distinct culinary regions:

  • Newar Feasts in Patan & Bandipur: Traditional Samay Baji platters with beaten rice (baji), spicy fire-roasted spiced buffalo (Chhoyela), black lentil patties (Wo / Bara), and sweet Juju Dhau curd.
  • Tibetan Staples in Kagbeni & Muktinath: Steamed dumplings (momos), hand-pulled noodle soup (Thukpa), roasted barley flour (tsampa), and warm salted yak-butter tea (su-chya).
  • Authentic Thakali Dining in Marpha: Steamed rice, slow-cooked black lentils tempered with wild Himalayan herb (jimbu), spicy fried potatoes, dried mustard greens (gundruk), and radish-timur pickle.
  • Mustang Apples: Freshly pressed cold apple juice, warm apple pies, and locally distilled Marpha apple brandy.

Alternative 10-Day Blueprints

Depending on whether you seek high mountain trekking or active wildlife safaris, substitute with these field-tested options:

Trekking Alternative Annapurna Base Camp (ABC) Trek (10 Days)

Fly to Pokhara, drive to Ghandruk/Jhinu, and trek up the Modi Khola canyon through bamboo forests into the 360-degree glaciated amphitheater of Annapurna Base Camp (4,130m); descend via natural hot springs at Jhinu Danda.

Kathmandu (2 Days) → Fly to Pokhara (2 Days) → Tansen/Palpa hill town (2 Days) → Chitwan National Park for walking rhinos safaris (2 Days) → Overland back to Kathmandu via Patan.

Acclimatization & Field Planning Rules

  • Never Drive Directly from Lowlands to Muktinath in One Day: Going from Pokhara (822m) straight to Muktinath (3,710m) in a single day creates severe altitude sickness risk. Always break your ascent by sleeping at Kagbeni (2,810m) or Jomsom first.
  • Mind the Afternoon Kali Gandaki Winds: Between 11:00 AM and 4:00 PM, fierce gale-force winds funnel up the river canyon from the south. Complete trail walks, flights, and open jeep legs early in the morning, and wear wrap-around sunglasses and a windproof buff.
  • Carry Warm Layers for Lower Mustang: Even during sunny spring and autumn months, Muktinath sits at 3,710 meters, where nighttime temperatures regularly drop below freezing. Bring a warm thermal base layer, a down jacket, and a fleece beanie.
  • Cash Realities: There are no working ATMs in Kagbeni or Muktinath, and Jomsom ATMs often experience network drops. Withdraw your entire trip budget in cash Nepali Rupees in Pokhara before boarding your flight.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need the expensive $500 Upper Mustang permit for this trip?

No. Jomsom, Kagbeni, Muktinath, and Marpha are located in Lower Mustang. They require only the standard ACAP permit (NPR 3,000) and TIMS card. The USD 500 Restricted Area Permit (RAP) is only required if you travel north past the checkpoint gate at Kagbeni into Upper Mustang.

Can I complete this entire 10-day trip without trekking?

Yes. The entire route is connected by motorable roads and domestic flights. You can reach the temple gate at Muktinath, the orchards of Marpha, and the alleys of Bandipur entirely by private 4WD jeep and car, making it ideal for families and travelers with limited mobility.

What happens if the Jomsom flight is cancelled due to weather?

Flights from Pokhara to Jomsom can experience morning weather delays. If a flight is cancelled, private 4WD Scorpio jeeps can drive you from Pokhara to Jomsom along the Beni highway (approx. 8–9 hours of scenic mountain driving).
