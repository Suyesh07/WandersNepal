21 days, used well

Three weeks supports real acclimatisation, a major trek and a meaningful cultural beginning and end.

Kathmandu ValleyDays 1–3
Selected trek with acclimatisationDays 4–16
Pokhara recovery and foodDays 17–19
Kathmandu buffer and departureDays 20–21

Route logic

KathmanduAnnapurna or Everest trekPokhara

Pace: Slow and complete. The sequence reduces backtracking and protects at least one flexible period where possible.

Budget framework

Use the Nepal trip cost calculator for a first range, then add international flights, permit fees, insurance and a weather contingency. Estimates are not quotes.

Make it yours

  • Remove a destination before removing every rest period.
  • Add wildlife only if you have enough time for two nights in Chitwan.
  • Add trekking only when the itinerary can absorb acclimatisation and weather.
  • Keep the final night close to the international departure airport.
