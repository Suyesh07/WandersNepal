7 Days in Nepal: The Complete Day-by-Day Masterplan

Seven days provides enough time to experience Nepal’s three classic geographic tiers: the historic hill basin of the Kathmandu Valley, the subtropical lake country beneath the Annapurnas, and the low-altitude jungles of the Terai plains. To avoid road exhaustion, this route utilizes a 25-minute flight from Kathmandu to Pokhara, followed by scenic daytime private overland legs down to Chitwan and back to the capital.

Day 1: Arrival in Kathmandu & Medieval Patan Courtyards

Day 01

Morning to Midday: Arrival & Patan Heritage. Land at Tribhuvan International Airport (TIA). Head south across the Bagmati River to check into your boutique base in Patan (Lalitpur). Stroll through Patan Durbar Square (entry: NPR 1,000), taking in the 17th-century stone Krishna Mandir, the Patan Museum in Keshav Narayan Chowk, and the tranquil courtyard of the Golden Temple (Kwa Bahal).

Late Afternoon & Sunset: Swayambhunath. Drive west to Swayambhunath (The Monkey Temple, entry: NPR 200). Climb the ancient stone steps for sunset views looking out over the Kathmandu Valley, where prayer flags drape over Buddhist stupas and Hindu shrines alike.

Overnight: Restored Newar boutique guesthouse in Patan or Kathmandu.

Day 2: Morning Boudhanath Kora, Flight to Pokhara & Lakefront Sunset

Day 02

Early Morning: Boudhanath Stupa. Arrive by 6:30 AM at Boudhanath (entry: NPR 400). Walk clockwise (kora) around the giant white dome alongside Tibetan pilgrims and monks. Enjoy an elevated rooftop breakfast overlooking prayer wheels and cedar incense smoke.

Midday: Flight to Pokhara. Transfer to TIA domestic terminal for the 25-minute flight to Pokhara Airport (PKR). Sit on the right side of the aircraft for views of the Langtang, Manaslu, and Annapurna massifs.

Afternoon & Dusk: Phewa Tal & Peace Pagoda. Check into your Lakeside hotel. Rent a hand-carved wooden boat (doonga) to row across Phewa Tal, then hike 45 minutes through Raniban forest to the gleaming white Shanti Stupa (World Peace Pagoda) for sunset over the lake and the spire of Machhapuchhare.

Overnight: Lakeside, Pokhara.

Day 3: Sarangkot Sunrise, Mountain Museum & Old Town

Day 03

Dawn: Sarangkot Himalayan Vista (1,600m). Take an early 30-minute drive or cable car to Sarangkot ridge before first light. Watch the dawn alpenglow sweep across Dhaulagiri (8,167m), Annapurna I (8,091m), and Machhapuchhare (6,993m).

Mid-Morning: Tandem Paragliding (Optional). Launch from Sarangkot or Toripani with a certified pilot, catching thermals over green terraces before landing near the lakefront.

Afternoon: Mountaineering History & Old Bazaar. Tour the International Mountain Museum to learn about 8,000-meter expeditions and Sherpa culture. Wander the 18th-century brick merchant homes of Purano Bazaar and Bindhyabasini Temple.

Dinner: Authentic Thakali Dal Bhat dinner in Central Lakeside. Overnight: Lakeside, Pokhara.

Day 4: Scenic Drive to Chitwan National Park & Tharu Village Walk

Day 04

Morning: Drive from Hills to Subtropical Plains. Depart Pokhara at 8:00 AM by private vehicle. Descend through green river gorges along the Seti and Narayani rivers to the southern lowlands of the Terai (approx. 140 km, 4.5–5 hours).

Afternoon: Tharu Village Immersion. Check into your lodge in Sauraha or Meghauli. Walk through a traditional Tharu indigenous village to observe mud-relief murals and sustainable agrarian living.

Dusk: Rapti River Sunset. Sit along the banks of the Rapti River with a cold drink, watching for wild marsh mugger crocodiles and rhinos emerging along the riverbed.

Overnight: Safari eco-lodge in Chitwan.

Day 5: Full-Day Chitwan Safari: Canoe, Walking Track & Open 4WD

Day 05

Early Morning: Silent Dugout Canoe Safari. Drift silently down the morning mist of the Rapti River in a hand-carved wooden canoe. Spot fish-eating gharials, marsh muggers, and kingfishers nesting along the mudbanks.

Mid-Morning: Guided Walking Bush Safari. Step onto the forest floor with two licensed naturalists, tracking one-horned rhinos, spotted deer (chital), and wild boars on foot through tall elephant grass.

Afternoon: Deep-Jungle 4WD Jeep Safari. Enter the core zone of Chitwan National Park (entry: NPR 2,000 + VAT) in an open-top 4WD vehicle. Traverse sal forests and oxbow lakes to track Greater One-Horned Rhinos, sloth bears, and the elusive Royal Bengal Tiger.

Evening: Cultural Tharu stick dance performance. Overnight: Safari eco-lodge in Chitwan.

Day 6: Return to Kathmandu & Evening Pashupatinath Aarti

Day 06

Morning: Birding & Return Transit. Enjoy early morning birdwatching along the riverbank (home to over 500 species). Then, take a 20-minute flight from nearby Bharatpur Airport (BHR) to Kathmandu, or take a private 5-hour drive via Narayangarh.

Afternoon: Rest & Thamel Craft Exploration. Check into your Kathmandu hotel. Wander Thamel’s quiet side-alleys to pick up singing bowls, pashminas, and loose-leaf Ilam tea.

Sunset & Night: Pashupatinath Sandhya Aarti. Visit Pashupatinath (entry: NPR 1,000). Stand along the holy Bagmati River terraces opposite the cremation ghats to witness the fire-lit evening Sandhya Aarti with chanting, bells, and brass oil lamps.

Overnight: Kathmandu or Patan.

Day 7: Medieval Bhaktapur Durbar Square & Departure

Day 07

Morning: Bhaktapur Durbar Square Exploration. Spend your final morning in the preserved medieval city-state of Bhaktapur (entry: NPR 1,800). Admire the five-tiered Nyatapola Temple, walk through open-air Pottery Square, and sample sweet Juju Dhau ("King Curd").

Midday: Final Departure Transfer. Bhaktapur sits only 12 km east of Tribhuvan International Airport (TIA). A 35-minute drive brings you directly to the departure terminal, bypassing central city traffic bottlenecks.

Departure: Check in at TIA 3 hours before international flight departure.

Regional Culinary Highlights

This one-week route covers three distinct culinary traditions:

  • Patan Newar Feasts: Sample Samay Baji with beaten rice (baji), fire-roasted spiced buffalo (Chhoyela), black lentil patties (Wo / Bara), and rice-flour crepes (Chatamari).
  • Pokhara Thakali Thali: Enjoy a mountain brass platter of rice, black lentils cooked with wild jimbu, spicy dry potatoes (alu tareko), and sharp radish pickle.
  • Chitwan Tharu Dishes: Savor indigenous Terai dishes like steamed rice dumplings (Bagiya), freshwater river snails (Ghonghi), and roasted spiced duck curry.
  • Bhaktapur Juju Dhau: Thick, rich buffalo-milk yogurt naturally sweetened and fermented in unglazed clay pots.

Alternative 7-Day Route Options

If your focus is active mountain trekking rather than a multi-stop road safari, substitute with this verified mountain route:

Mountain Alternative Langtang Valley Glacial Trek (7 Days)

Drive from Kathmandu to Syabrubesi on Day 1; trek up the forest gorge to Lama Hotel on Day 2; reach Langtang Village on Day 3; arrive at Kyanjin Gompa (3,870m) on Day 4; climb Kyanjin Ri (4,773m) and retrace on Days 5–6; drive back to Kathmandu on Day 7.

Foothill Alternative Ghorepani Poon Hill & Ghandruk (7 Days)

Fly to Pokhara; trek through oak and rhododendron forests to Ghorepani; catch sunrise over Dhaulagiri from Poon Hill (3,210m); descend through traditional Gurung stone villages in Ghandruk; return to Pokhara and Kathmandu.

Essential Planning Rules for 7 Days

  • Fly One Leg to Save Time: Always fly between Kathmandu and Pokhara. This single 25-minute domestic flight saves a full day of highway travel.
  • Choose Ethical Safaris in Chitwan: Opt for walking safaris, river canoe paddles, and electric/open-top 4WD jeep tracks rather than captive elephant rides.
  • Pack for Diverse Climates: You will move from cool ridge mornings in Sarangkot (8–14°C) to warm, humid jungle weather in Chitwan (24–30°C). Bring lightweight quick-dry layers, a warm fleece, and neutral bush-toned clothing for safaris.
  • Keep the Final Night in the Valley: Arrive back in the Kathmandu Valley on Day 6 to avoid transport delays before your international flight on Day 7.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is 7 days enough to see Nepal?

Yes. Seven days is the ideal timeframe for the classic Golden Triangle (Kathmandu, Pokhara, and Chitwan). You will experience Nepal's historical UNESCO architecture, Himalayan mountain views, and wildlife safaris without rushing.

Do I need malaria medication for Chitwan?

Chitwan is considered low-risk for malaria, but mosquito repellent with DEET is strongly recommended for dusk walks and canoe safaris, especially between April and October. Consult a travel medicine clinic before departure.

What is the best season for this 7-day trip?

October through April is ideal. Autumn (October–November) offers clear mountain views and dry safari trails. Spring (March–April) brings warm weather and good tiger-tracking conditions in Chitwan as forest grasses thin out.
