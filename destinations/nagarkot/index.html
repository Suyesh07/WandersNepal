Nagarkot Nepal: Complete Travel Guide & Best Things to Do

Nagarkot is a tranquil hill station situated at the northeastern side of the Kathmandu Valley basin. The peaceful village is surrounded by green pine forests, clean mountain air, and large terraces. While the Kathmandu Valley is very busy and dusty, Nagarkot is a place where one can take a break and relax.

Nagarkot at a Glance: Essential Travel Overview

Nagarkot Geography, Altitude & Weather (2,175m)

Nagarkot is located at a height of 2,175 meters (7,135 feet) above sea level. Since it is much higher than Kathmandu (which is located at an altitude of around 1,400 meters), the weather here is different from that of Kathmandu. Nagarkot generally has a cooler temperature of 5°C to 8°C compared to Kathmandu. The climate is nice and windy during the day, but the crisp chill of the mountains kicks in as soon as the day ends.

The setup of the town is along the top ridge of the mountain and is connected by a curved blacktop road that rises up from Bhaktapur. In the morning, dense white clouds usually sit low in the valleys, giving the hilltop the appearance of being an island above the ocean of white fog.

Best Time to Visit Nagarkot: Seasons for Clear Visibility

The most important factor when visiting Nagarkot is visibility. If the sky is cloudy, you will only see white fog instead of the Himalayan peaks.

Season Months Atmospheric Conditions & Mountain Visibility
Autumn (Peak Clarity) Oct – Dec This is the best time of the year. The monsoon rains have cleared the dust from the air, leaving dry, crisp blue skies. Mountain visibility is at its absolute best.
Spring (Warm & Floral) Mar – Apr The weather is warm, days are clear, and the surrounding pine forests fill with blooming rhododendrons and wild flowers. Afternoon haze can sometimes roll in, but mornings are usually very clear.
Winter (Clear & Chilly) Jan – Feb Skies are often clear and sunny, giving you sharp mountain views. However, the mornings and nights are freezing, so you must carry heavy warm clothes.
Monsoon (Clouds & Mist) Jun – Sep This season is not recommended for mountain views. Heavy rains bring cloudy skies, mud, and road landslides, completely hiding the Himalayan range.

Top Highlights: 5 Nagarkot Experiences You Cannot Miss

  • Watch the sunrise turn the Himalayan mountain range from cold grey to glowing gold.
  • Spot the distant tip of Mount Everest along with the Langtang and Ganesh Himal ranges.
  • Take a tandem paragliding flight over the terraced green valleys starting from Lapsiphedi.
  • Walk the easy downhill trail through local pine forests to the ancient Changu Narayan Temple.
  • Relax on a hotel rooftop or private balcony with hot tea, far away from the city traffic.

The Famous Nagarkot Sunrise View

Spotting the Everest "Speck" & The Langtang Range

Nagarkot is well-known for providing views of 8 out of 13 mountain ranges in Nepal through one location. You can see the Annapurna, Manaslu, Ganesh Himal, Langtang, Jugal, Rolwaling, Mahalangur, and Numbur mountain ranges from one place.

Tourism companies around the world tell tourists that they will see Mount Everest from a very short distance. However, Mount Everest is located about 138 kilometers from Nagarkot. On a clear day, Mount Everest can be seen on the horizon looking small. To see Mount Everest clearly, you should take binoculars with you.

When the sun rises in Nagarkot, the real views are of the Ganesh Himal and Langtang Range. These are huge mountains located very close to Nagarkot. When the sun rises in the morning, these mountains are lit in bright colors.

Nagarkot View Tower vs. Your Hotel Balcony

It is commonly suggested that to catch a glimpse of the sunrise, one must get up at 4:30 AM, trek for an hour in the dark, and go to the Nagarkot View Tower (also known as Geodetic Tower).

This tower is located on the apex of the ridge at an altitude of 2,195m, making a breathtaking 360-degree view possible. Unfortunately, this tower tends to be very crowded, filled with noisy tourists and tour buses bringing people in with their cameras. The time before dawn is unbearably cold, making this trip less enjoyable.

Here is a local recommendation: there are numerous hotels and guesthouses located on the eastern side of Nagarkot with open flat roofs and private balconies which are perfectly positioned to capture sunrise views. It is often possible to watch sunrise directly from the comfort of your room in your warm robe without having to undertake the journey to the crowded View Tower. However, if your hotel or guesthouse is not on the eastern side, then you might end up a lot less comfortable.

Top Things to Do & Places to Visit in Nagarkot

Paragliding in Nagarkot: Everest & Langtang Views

Pokhara is known for its breathtaking paragliding experience over Lake Phewa. For those who want the same experience, Nagarkot is closer to Kathmandu and offers this remarkable activity. Enjoy soaring above the quiet fields and pine-studded hills, with the snow-clad peaks glaring at a distance.

Paragliding takes place from mid-September to mid-April when the winds are relatively steady, that is, between 8 am and 3 pm. The paragliding take-off point at Lapsiphedi, which lies 1,905 meters above sea level, is a short ride from the city. The actual flight lasts 20 to 30 minutes and takes you above the valley of 1,307 meters height.

The Panoramic Nature Trail & Tamang Village Walk

The Nagarkot Panoramic Hiking Trail is suitable for anaysis of nature fans, as it is easy to follow. The hike takes 3 to 3.5 hours. During the hike, one can get away from the traffic of the main road and enter quiet pine woods with the real mountain breeze and chirping birds.

The trail passes by Kartike and Tamang villages. Moreover, the walking route gives an insight into life in the suburbs of the capital. It is possible to see traditional mud-and-stone houses, children in local streets, and farmers working in terraced fields growing mustard, potatoes, and corn.

Nagarkot Waterfall & Buddha Peace Park

If you wish to have an interesting trip in the afternoons, then Muhan Pokhari or the Nagarkot Waterfall is a nice natural spot to explore. Muhan Pokhari is a natural spring that runs down the hills and supplies drinking water to the valley down below. It is a popular spot for local people to relax and enjoy the cool waters.

Another nice destination close to Nagarkot is the Buddha Peace Park. It is a gorgeous park with a huge golden statue of Lord Buddha in the meditation posture (Bhumisparsha Mudra). Positioned on a hill, the park gives an excellent view to other green valleys nearby, providing a good place for taking pictures and resting. If you have some time to spare, you can also take a cab towards Sanga to see the Kailashnath Mahadev Statue which is among the highest statues of Shiva in Asia.

Nagarkot Hiking Trails & Nature Walks

The Nagarkot to Changu Narayan Hike (UNESCO Trail)

The hike from Nagarkot to Changu Narayan Temple is one of the finest day trips to take in the Kathmandu Valley. The distance you will cover is about 12 kilometers and it will take approximately 3.5 to 4.5 hours.

As you start from the high elevation of Nagarkot at 2,175 meters to Changu Narayan at 1,540 meters, the trail you will follow is almost completely downhill. You will walk through peaceful pine forest in Tel Kot and along open dirt paths dotted with traditional farming settlements.

At Changu Narayan Temple you will end your hike in a site listed on UNESCO World Heritage Site list as the oldest Hindu temple in Nepal. The temple, which was built during the reign of Lichhavi in 464 AD, is known for its ancient stone sculptures and detailed wooden carvings.

Chisapani to Nagarkot Trek (Kathmandu Valley Rim)

For those looking for a long hike, the trail from Chisapani to Nagarkot is one of the best in the Kathmandu Valley Rim trek. The hike can be about 18 to 20 kilometers long and takes 6 to 7 hours of continuous walking.

The trek starts inside Shivapuri Nagarjun National Park (usually accessed from Sundarijal). You will be heading through the dense subtropical forest of rhododendron and oak, before coming out to the open ridges that follow the rim of the valley. It is a quiet and serene trail that leads directly to the hotels in Nagarkot, making it perfect for a weekend hike.

Nagarkot Travel Tips: Practical Logistics

How to Reach Nagarkot from Kathmandu (Distance & Transport)

Nagarkot is found at a distance of 32 km from Kathmandu, in a northeast direction. You have two main options to reach Nagarkot depending upon your budget and style of travel. For a broader overview of moving across Nepal, see our domestic transportation guide.

The most straightforward and the quickest way to reach Nagarkot is to hire a taxi from the airport or Thamel. The travel would take at least 1.5 hours when travelling on the Araniko Highway through Bhaktapur and then the hilly stretch. On an average, the cost one way from Thamel to Nagarkot would be between NPR 2,500 to NPR 3,500.

To travel on a tight budget, you can take the local public bus. There is not any direct public bus to reach Nagarkot from the central Kathmandu. The first step would be to take the local bus from Ratna Park or Bagbazar to Kamalbinayak Bus Park in Bhaktapur (costing around NPR 40 to NPR 50, taking around 45 mins). After arriving at Kamalbinayak, you can switch the local bus going to Nagarkot (costing around NPR 70 to NPR 80, taking around 1.5 hours).

Entry Fee Note: The foreign travelers around the world have to pay NPR 339 as municipal tourist entry fee at the checkpost before entering the main town.

Kathmandu to Nagarkot Day Trip vs. Overnight Stay

Nagarkot can be reached easily from Kathmandu in a single day drive in a private car starting at 4.00 AM to be present for the dawn, enjoy breakfast there, and return by 12.00 PM.

However, it is good to spend overnight in Nagarkot. A overnight spent there would allow one to reach the place by afternoon, witness the magical sunset on the mountains, sleep peacefully in the tranquil air, and find out right on the balcony in the morning without having received a rude awakening in the form of a bumpy ride on dark and twisting mountain roads at 4.00 AM.

Altitude Packing Warning: Why You Need a Fleece Jacket

Although warm months of the year like April or October are experienced in Nagarkot, the climate can be very chilly at night due to its altitude of 2,175 meters. Centrally heated accommodation is often unavailable in budget and mid-range hotels in Nagarkot.

One should always ensure that a warm fleece or light down jacket is packed, even if it feels like it is extremely hot when leaving Kathmandu. Windproof jackets are also essential in order to combat the early morning chill and comfortable walking shoes must be worn for when hiking on the trails. Due to extremely cold mountain air phone batteries tend to run down quickly so it is advised that a portable power bank be carried along.

Frequently Asked Questions About Visiting Nagarkot

Is Nagarkot worth visiting?

Absolutely, Nagarkot is a recommended place to visit. It is the easiest place to go and enjoy breathtaking views of the Himalayas, the fresh mountain air and walks in nature without having to book domestic flights or carry heavy trekking bags.

Can you see Mount Everest from Nagarkot?

Yes, you can see Mt. Everest from Nagarkot on clear days in October, November and December. From far across the Mahalangur range in the east, you will see Everest as a tiny, pointy peak. Since it is located 138 km away, it is quite small, so a pair of binoculars would come in handy.

What is the best time to visit Nagarkot?

The ideal period to visit Nagarkot is between October and December as the weather conditions are perfect for excellent mountain views. Additionally, the months of March and April are nice for seeing rhododendron flowers blooming.

How do I get to Nagarkot from Kathmandu?

You can hire a private taxi direct from Kathmandu, which takes about 1.5 hours and costs between NPR 2,500 and NPR 3,500. Public buses run from Ratna Park to Bhaktapur and then local buses from Kamalbinayak up to Nagarkot. This is a good option for budget travellers.

How hard is the hike from Nagarkot to Changu Narayan?

The Nagarkot to Changu Narayan hike is easy to moderate and is suitable for beginners and families. It is 12 km long and takes about 3.5 to 4.5 hours to complete. There is very little uphill climbing since the trail descends from the high ridge to the valley floor.

How many days should I spend in Nagarkot?

1 night and 2 days in Nagarkot is perfect. This leaves you plenty of time to watch the sunset, have a quiet dinner, see the sunrise over the mountains, and take a scenic village walk or do the Changu Narayan day hike before returning.
