Nepal Destinations: Across Seven Geographic Zones

Nepal spans an elevation gradient from 60 meters in the Terai lowlands to 8,848 meters at Mount Everest. Each region has distinct weather windows, trail conditions, permit requirements, and cultural traditions. Use our verified regional notes to choose your base hubs deliberately.

Choose a starting point.

01

Bagmati · Living Heritage

Kathmandu Valley

Three historic royal cities, intricate Newar courtyards, and centuries of living ritual—best explored slowly on foot.
02

Gandaki · Lakes & Adventure

Pokhara

A sub-tropical lakeside haven serving as the primary staging ground for Annapurna expeditions, day hikes, and boating.
03

Koshi · High Himalaya

Everest Region

The Khumbu heartland: Sherpa mountain settlements, high-altitude Buddhist monasteries, and routes to Everest Base Camp.
04

Gandaki · Village Trekking

Annapurna Region

Subtropical terraced hills, Gurung villages, and alpine sanctuaries culminating at Thorong La pass.
05

Terai Lowlands · Wildlife & Tharu Culture

Chitwan

Dense riverine jungles home to one-horned rhinos, Bengal tigers, gharials, and indigenous Tharu heritage.
06

Lumbini Province · Sacred Heritage

Lumbini

The UNESCO birthplace of Gautama Buddha: a peaceful zone of international monasteries, gardens, and archaeological sites.
07

Gandaki · Restricted High Desert

Upper Mustang

An arid trans-Himalayan Tibetan kingdom marked by whitewashed fortresses, centuries-old sky caves, and canyon trails.
08

Karnali · Remote Lake & Pines

Rara Lake

Nepal's largest alpine lake, resting peacefully among pine-covered ridges far beyond commercial tourism paths.
09

Bagmati Himal · Accessible Valley

Langtang

A compact mountain valley easily reached by overland transport from Kathmandu, rich in Tamang hospitality and glaciers.
10

Gandaki Himal · Restricted Circuit

Manaslu

A wilderness circuit tracing the Budhi Gandaki river gorge to Tibetan-border settlements and Larkya La pass (5,106m).
11

Langtang, Bagmati · Sacred Alpine Tarns

Gosaikunda

A cluster of oligotrophic glacial lakes at 4,380m venerated in Hindu and Buddhist mythology and frequented by ridge trekkers.
12

Gandaki Hills · Preserved Newar Bazaar

Bandipur

A tranquil, vehicle-free hilltop town preserving 18th-century Newar merchant homes and expansive mountain panoramas.
13

Kathmandu Valley Rim · Sunrise Viewpoint

Nagarkot

An accessible forested ridge on the eastern edge of the valley offering views stretching from Annapurna to Everest in clear weather.
14

Madhesh Lowlands · Mithila Art & Heritage

Janakpur

The cultural nucleus of Mithila civilization, famed for the palace-like Janaki Mandir, sacred ponds, and folk painting.
15

Koshi Hills · Eastern Tea Country

Ilam

Terraced tea plantations, cool misty ridgelines, and authentic rural homestays in Nepal's eastern agricultural hills.
16

Mustang, Gandaki · Pilgrimage Valley

Muktinath

A sacred sanctuary at 3,710m uniting Hindu and Buddhist pilgrims above the windswept Kali Gandaki gorge.
17

Lumbini Hills · Traditional Artisan Town

Tansen / Palpa

A steep hill station celebrated for woven Dhaka fabric, bronze crafts, Magar cultural roots, and Shreenagar hill vistas.
18

Sudurpashchim · High Moors & Forest

Khaptad

Gentle sub-alpine grasslands, dense fir forests, and meditation hermitages in far-western Nepal's quietest conservation plateau.
19

Koshi Himal · Wilderness High Trekking

Kanchenjunga

Demanding expedition-grade approaches to the world's third-highest peak through rhododendron forests and remote Limbu hamlets.
20

Karnali Himal · Trans-Himalayan Lake

Dolpo / Phoksundo

Spectacular turquoise alpine waters and ancient pre-Buddhist Bonpo monasteries across Nepal's most secluded plateau.
