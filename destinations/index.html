Twenty ways into Nepal

Destinations

Start with one of twenty major destination hubs, then connect it to a route, trek or realistic itinerary.

Choose a starting point.

01

Bagmati · Living heritage

Kathmandu Valley

Three historic cities, layered neighbourhoods and living ritual—best understood one courtyard at a time.
02

Gandaki · Lakes & adventure

Pokhara

A lakeside city where Himalayan mornings open into ridge walks, paddling, neighbourhood food and unhurried afternoons.
03

Koshi · High Himalaya

Everest Region

The Khumbu is not one viewpoint but a lived mountain landscape of Sherpa villages, monasteries and high valleys.
04

Gandaki · Village trekking

Annapurna Region

A web of trails links subtropical valleys, rhododendron forest, Gurung villages and the high desert beyond Thorong La.
05

Madhesh–Bagmati lowlands · Wildlife & Tharu culture

Chitwan

Riverine forest and tall grasslands reveal a lowland Nepal of rhinos, birds, gharials and Tharu heritage.
06

Lumbini Province · Sacred heritage

Lumbini

The birthplace of the Buddha is a quiet landscape of archaeology, gardens and living monastic traditions.
07

Gandaki · Restricted high desert

Upper Mustang

A trans-Himalayan world of walled settlements, cave complexes, wind-carved cliffs and Tibetan Buddhist culture.
08

Karnali · Remote lake & forest

Rara Lake

Nepal’s largest lake rests among pine ridges in Karnali, far beyond the country’s familiar travel corridors.
09

Bagmati Himal · Accessible mountain valley

Langtang

A compact Himalayan valley north of Kathmandu, rebuilt through community effort and best travelled with time to listen.
10

Gandaki Himal · Restricted circuit

Manaslu

A demanding circuit follows the Budhi Gandaki from warm valleys to Tibetan-influenced villages and Larkya La.
11

Langtang, Bagmati · Sacred alpine lakes

Gosaikunda

A chain of sacred alpine lakes sits above forest and Tamang villages on a high route between Langtang and Helambu.
12

Gandaki hills · Historic hill town

Bandipur

A preserved hill bazaar turns the Kathmandu–Pokhara journey into a place worth pausing.
13

Kathmandu Valley rim · Himalayan viewpoints

Nagarkot

A forested ridge gives the Kathmandu Valley breathing room—and, in clear weather, a long Himalayan horizon.
14

Madhesh · Mithila culture & pilgrimage

Janakpur

A sacred city of bright temple architecture, Mithila painting and the living story of Sita.
15

Koshi hills · Tea country

Ilam

Green ridges, tea gardens and misty market towns introduce Nepal’s quieter eastern hills.
16

Mustang, Gandaki · Pilgrimage & high desert

Muktinath

A sacred Himalayan site brings Hindu and Buddhist traditions together above the Kali Gandaki valley.
17

Lumbini hills · Hill town & craft

Tansen / Palpa

A steep, lived-in hill town of courtyards, metalwork, woven dhaka and far-reaching ridge views.
18

Far West · High meadows & forest

Khaptad

Rolling high meadows, forest and sacred places form one of far-west Nepal’s least hurried landscapes.
19

Koshi Himal · Remote high trekking

Kanchenjunga

Long approaches and vast valleys lead toward the world’s third-highest mountain in Nepal’s far east.
20

Karnali Himal · Remote lake & trans-Himalaya

Dolpo / Phoksundo

Turquoise Phoksundo and the austere valleys beyond open one of Nepal’s most remote journeys.
