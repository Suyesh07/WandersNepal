Overview

Kanchenjunga is a major expedition-style trek rather than a quick alternative to Everest. The north and south base-camp routes cross isolated settlements and protected landscapes, requiring current permits, licensed support and robust contingency planning.

Why visit?

Reason 01One of Nepal’s wildest major trekking regions
Reason 02North and south approaches with distinct landscapes
Reason 03Deep immersion in the far-eastern Himalaya

Things to do

  • Trek to Pangpema area
  • Approach the south base camp
  • Cross linking passes when safe
  • Walk through cardamom country
  • Learn Limbu and highland cultures
  • Watch for red panda habitat signs
  • Use long acclimatisation plans
  • Carry multiple contingency days

Places to visit

  • Taplejung
  • Sekathum
  • Ghunsa
  • Kambachen
  • Pangpema
  • Tseram

How to get there

The approach combines flights or long roads to eastern Nepal with jeeps and trekking. Landslides and weather can alter trailheads; use current operator intelligence.

Changing information: schedules, roadheads and fares vary. Last verified September 2026; confirm with the operator and relevant authority before travel.

Where to stay

Basic teahouses exist on many stages but capacity and supplies are limited. Some itineraries need additional logistical support.

Food

Simple local staples dominate. Avoid oversized imported menus and carry carefully planned trail nutrition.

Culture

The route crosses Limbu, Rai, Sherpa and Tibetan-influenced communities. A local guide is central to context and communication.

Safety

Distance, altitude, weather and limited rescue access make this a serious undertaking. Comprehensive evacuation cover and conservative decisions are essential.

Responsible travel

Keep group size modest, protect forest habitat and ensure staff have appropriate equipment and insurance.

Frequently asked questions

How many days should I spend in Kanchenjunga?

18–26 days is a useful starting range. Add time for transport, weather and any altitude adjustment.

When is the best time to visit Kanchenjunga?

The broad planning window is April–May · October–November, but local weather and road or trail conditions should be checked close to travel.

Can I travel to Kanchenjunga independently?

It depends on access, permits and current guide regulations. Restricted and remote regions need registered support; city and lowland hubs are more flexible.

What should I verify before leaving?

Recheck transport, entry or permit requirements, weather, insurance coverage and the latest local safety advice.
