Overview

Lower Dolpo’s Phoksundo Lake can be reached on a shorter trek, while Upper Dolpo requires a much longer restricted-area expedition. Both demand respect for fragile logistics, Bon and Tibetan Buddhist cultural landscapes, and the distance from rapid assistance.

Why visit?

Reason 01The extraordinary colour and setting of Phoksundo Lake
Reason 02Distinct Bon and Tibetan Buddhist heritage
Reason 03Routes that feel genuinely removed from major corridors

Things to do

  • Walk to Phoksundo Lake
  • Visit Ringmo respectfully
  • Watch the waterfall viewpoint
  • Trek high passes with support
  • Learn Bon cultural context
  • Camp with minimal impact
  • Build weather contingency
  • Choose route length honestly

Places to visit

  • Juphal
  • Dunai
  • Chhepka
  • Ringmo
  • Phoksundo Lake
  • Shey area

How to get there

Most journeys use a combination of flights to Nepalgunj and Juphal, then trekking. Flight chains are disruption-prone, while overland alternatives are long.

Where to stay

Basic lodges serve parts of the Phoksundo route; longer Upper Dolpo itineraries may require organised camping and full logistics.

Food

Supplies are limited and costly to transport. Eat local staples where available and plan group provisions without waste.

Culture

Ringmo and upper valleys are living communities with sacred sites. Always seek permission for interiors, ceremonies and close portrait photography.

Safety

Remoteness, high passes, cold and limited communications demand experienced leadership and a formal evacuation plan.

Responsible travel

Pack out waste, protect scarce fuelwood and water, and follow restricted-area requirements exactly.

Frequently asked questions

How many days should I spend in Dolpo / Phoksundo?

8–28 days is a useful starting range. Add time for transport, weather and any altitude adjustment.

When is the best time to visit Dolpo / Phoksundo?

The broad planning window is May–October, but local weather and road or trail conditions should be checked close to travel.

Can I travel to Dolpo / Phoksundo independently?

It depends on access, permits and current guide regulations. Restricted and remote regions need registered support; city and lowland hubs are more flexible.

What should I verify before leaving?

Recheck transport, entry or permit requirements, weather, insurance coverage and the latest local safety advice.
