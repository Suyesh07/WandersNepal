Overview

The Manaslu Circuit is often described as an alternative to Annapurna, but it has its own character: a long approach through river gorges, culturally distinct upper settlements and a serious high-pass crossing. Restricted-area regulations require supported planning.

Why visit?

Reason 01Major landscape change over one continuous route
Reason 02Less trafficked than Nepal’s best-known circuits
Reason 03Strong cultural progression into the upper valley

Things to do

  • Follow the Budhi Gandaki gorge
  • Acclimatise around Samagaun
  • Visit Pungyen Gompa area
  • View Manaslu from upper valleys
  • Cross Larkya La in safe conditions
  • Observe changing architecture
  • Add Tsum Valley if time allows
  • Use experienced local support

Places to visit

  • Machha Khola
  • Jagat
  • Namrung
  • Samagaun
  • Samdo
  • Dharapani

How to get there

Road transport from Kathmandu reaches changing trailheads along the Budhi Gandaki. The route exits toward Dharapani, linking by jeep and bus toward Besisahar. Road condition checks are essential.

Changing information: schedules, roadheads and fares vary. Last verified September 2026; confirm with the operator and relevant authority before travel.

Where to stay

Teahouse infrastructure exists on the circuit but is simpler than in Annapurna. Peak-season capacity can be tight near the pass.

Food

Menus narrow at altitude. Dal bhat, porridge, potatoes, soups and noodles are reliable; carry personal trail snacks sparingly.

Culture

Upper villages have strong Tibetan Buddhist traditions. Follow guide advice for monasteries, mani walls and photography.

Safety

Landslides, altitude, cold and the pass crossing require conservative decisions. Current guide and permit rules must be confirmed before departure.

Responsible travel

Respect porter loads, avoid shortcutting fragile slopes and keep groups compact in small villages.

Frequently asked questions

How many days should I spend in Manaslu?

14–18 days is a useful starting range. Add time for transport, weather and any altitude adjustment.

When is the best time to visit Manaslu?

The broad planning window is March–April · October–November, but local weather and road or trail conditions should be checked close to travel.

Can I travel to Manaslu independently?

It depends on access, permits and current guide regulations. Restricted and remote regions need registered support; city and lowland hubs are more flexible.

What should I verify before leaving?

Recheck transport, entry or permit requirements, weather, insurance coverage and the latest local safety advice.
