Everest Base Camp Trek

This high-altitude journey goes through Sherpa country to the foot of the Khumbu Icefall. It's paced for acclimatisation, not speed, along the classic Everest trekking route. This route passes through Sagarmatha National Park, with its iconic landscapes, and also ancient Buddhist monasteries. There are panoramic viewpoints of the Himalayas too.

Everest Base Camp Trek Itinerary

|

Day 1: Arrival in Kathmandu (1,400 m / 4,593 ft)

Easy

Your journey starts with landing at Tribhuvan International Airport in Kathmandu, followed by a transfer to your hotel. Use this day to rest, meet your guide and get ready for the trek ahead. Overnight at a 3-star hotel in Kathmandu.

Day 2: Kathmandu to Phakding via Lukla (2,610 m / 8,563 ft)

Easy

The trek officially begins with a 35-minute flight from Kathmandu to Lukla (2,860 m / 9,383 ft), the gateway to the Khumbu region. Alternatively, drive 5–6 hours by shared tourist vehicle to Manthali/Ramechhap (474 m / 1,555 ft), followed by a 20-minute flight to Lukla. From there, walk 3–4 hours to Phakding, passing Sherpa villages and mountain scenery. Overnight at a guesthouse in Phakding.

Day 3: Phakding to Namche Bazaar (3,440 m / 11,286 ft)

Hard

Today's 5–6 hour walk takes you into Sagarmatha National Park and across several suspension bridges over the Dudh Koshi River, ending in Namche Bazaar, the busy trading town that serves as the hub of the Khumbu. Overnight at a guesthouse in Namche Bazaar.

Day 4: Acclimatization Day in Namche Bazaar (3,440 m / 11,286 ft)

Moderate

This is a rest day built around altitude adjustment, with an optional 4–5 hour hike up to Hotel Everest View (3,880 m / 12,730 ft) for your first real look at Everest. Overnight at a guesthouse in Namche Bazaar.

Day 5: Namche Bazaar to Tengboche (3,860 m / 12,664 ft)

Moderate

A 5–6 hour walk through rhododendron forest brings you to Tengboche, known for its monastery and mountain views, including Ama Dablam and Everest. Overnight at a guesthouse in Tengboche.

Day 6: Tengboche to Dingboche (4,410 m / 14,469 ft)

Moderate

The trail climbs into more open terrain on this 5–6 hour stretch to Dingboche, a small village surrounded by some of the highest peaks in the world. Overnight at a guesthouse in Dingboche.

Day 7: Acclimatization Day in Dingboche (4,410 m / 14,469 ft)

Moderate

Take a rest day, with the option to hike to Nangkartsang Peak (5,083 m / 16,676 ft) for views of Makalu, Lhotse and the Imja Valley. The round trip takes about 4–5 hours. Overnight at a guesthouse in Dingboche.

Day 8: Dingboche to Lobuche (4,910 m / 16,109 ft)

Hard

This 5–6 hour day crosses rugged high-altitude terrain. Pass memorials for climbers who died attempting Everest, a quiet reminder of the mountain's scale. Overnight at a guesthouse in Lobuche.

Day 9: Lobuche to Everest Base Camp (5,364 m / 17,598 ft) via Gorak Shep

Hard

This is the day many trekkers have been waiting for. Trek from Lobuche to Gorak Shep, continue to Everest Base Camp, then return to Gorak Shep for the night. Allow 8–9 hours for the trek and return. Overnight at a guesthouse in Gorak Shep.

Day 10: Kala Patthar (about 5,550 m / 18,209 ft) to Pheriche (4,240 m / 13,911 ft)

Hard

An early start takes you to Kala Patthar for sunrise, widely considered one of the best viewpoints of Everest's summit on the trek, before descending to Pheriche. Allow roughly 7–8 hours in total. Overnight at a guesthouse in Pheriche.

Day 11: Pheriche to Namche Bazaar (3,440 m / 11,286 ft)

Hard

From here, the route is mostly downhill. Walk 7–8 hours back through familiar villages to Namche Bazaar. Overnight at a guesthouse in Namche Bazaar.

Day 12: Namche Bazaar to Lukla (2,860 m / 9,383 ft)

Hard

The last full day of trekking covers 7–8 hours back to Lukla, where the walking part of the journey wraps up. Overnight at a guesthouse in Lukla.

Day 13: Lukla to Kathmandu

Moderate

A 35-minute flight takes you from Lukla back to Kathmandu. Alternatively, take a 20-minute flight to Manthali/Ramechhap, followed by a 5–6 hour drive to Kathmandu by shared tourist vehicle. Overnight at a 3-star hotel in Kathmandu.

Day 14: Final Departure

Easy

Transfer to Tribhuvan International Airport for your flight home, bringing the trip to a close.

Shorter Everest View Trek Itinerary: 8 Days

Prefer a shorter Khumbu trek without continuing to Everest Base Camp? This Everest View itinerary focuses on Namche Bazaar, Khumjung, Tengboche and the classic Everest viewpoints.

Day 1: Arrival in Kathmandu (1,365 m)

Arrival

Settle into your hotel, meet the trekking guide and complete a final gear check before the mountain journey begins.

Day 2: Fly to Lukla and trek to Phakding (2,652 m)

4 hours

Take an early morning flight to Lukla, then descend alongside the Dudh Koshi through pine forests and small settlements to Phakding.

Day 3: Trek to Namche Bazaar (3,443 m)

7 hours

Cross suspension bridges into Sagarmatha National Park and climb through pine and cedar forest to Namche Bazaar.

Day 4: Namche viewpoint and Khumjung (3,880 m)

Acclimatization

Hike to the Syangboche ridge for views of Everest, Lhotse and Ama Dablam, then visit the Sherpa village, monastery and museum at Khumjung.

Day 5: Trek to Tengboche (3,868 m)

5–6 hours

Climb through rhododendron and fir forest to Tengboche, whose monastery sits beneath views of Ama Dablam and Everest.

Day 6: Return to Namche Bazaar (3,443 m)

5 hours

Descend through Pungki Thanka and Kyangjuma before returning to Namche for a final evening in the Khumbu.

Day 7: Descend to Lukla (2,860 m)

7 hours

Follow the Dudh Koshi past bridges and villages on the final long descent to Lukla.

Day 8: Fly back to Kathmandu

Flight day

Take an early morning scenic flight from Lukla back to Kathmandu and rest after the trek.

Everest Base Camp Helicopter Tour

Depart early in the morning, when skies are often clearer for mountain views. A comfortable 3–4-seat helicopter takes you over a panorama that may include Manaslu, Ganesh Himal, Langtang, Jugal Himal, Cho Oyu, Everest and Makalu.

After about an hour, the tour may stop in Lukla for refreshments before continuing toward Namche Bazaar and Everest View Hotel. Depending on weather, aircraft performance and permissions, the route may include a brief stop near Kala Patthar (5,545 m / 18,192 ft) and a flyover near Everest Base Camp (about 5,364 m / 17,598 ft), with views of the Khumbu Icefall and glacier.

The return may include a short stop near Tengboche Monastery, surrounded by forest and high peaks, followed by views over Namche Bazaar and Lukla before landing in Kathmandu. A vehicle transfer returns you to your hotel. Stops and flight paths can change with weather and operating conditions.

Everest Base Camp Trek FAQ

How high is Everest Base Camp?

Everest Base Camp is about 5,364 m (17,598 ft) above sea level.

What should I pack for the Everest Base Camp Trek?

Pack a backpack, hiking boots, down jacket, rain or windproof jacket, camp shoes, socks, trekking trousers, thermal wear, shirts or T-shirts, gloves, hat, sunglasses, sleeping bag, water bottle and purifier, trekking poles, gaiters, microspikes, map, other personal essentials, a medical kit, and clinic and health-post information.

How much money should I take on the Everest Base Camp Trek?

A standard all-inclusive 12- to 14-day guided Everest Base Camp trek package typically costs between $1,200 and $1,800 USD, or NPR 160,000 to NPR 240,000. Trekkers should also budget an extra $300 to $500 USD in physical cash for personal expenses on the trail.
