Field Corrections & Editorial Submissions

Mountain roads expand, bridge washouts happen during the monsoon, and local checkpoint permit fees evolve. We rely on active field observations from independent trekkers, certified guides, and local lodge owners across Nepal.

  • Trail Notice Updates: Report washed-out bridges, rockfall hazards, or trail realignments on our Field Corrections Page.
  • Media & Syndication: For publishing rights, high-resolution imagery licensing, or editorial partnerships, email our desk with details of your publication.
  • Guide & Agency Verification: We do not accept paid placements for unlicensed operators. All agency evaluations adhere strictly to our transparent Editorial Policy.

Emergency & Mountain Rescue Contacts

Please Note: Wanders Nepal is an independent research and editorial guide, not an on-demand emergency rescue dispatch service. If you or a member of your party is experiencing severe altitude illness (AMS/HAPE/HACE) or an accident in the field, contact the official emergency services immediately:

  • Tourist Police Nepal (Kathmandu Toll-Free): 1144 or +977 1 4247041
  • Himalayan Rescue Association (HRA): +977 1 4440292 / 4440293
  • Nepal Police Emergency Hotline: 100
  • Civil Aviation Authority Search & Rescue Coordination: +977 1 4113000

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you arrange private guided tours directly?

We provide independent route intel, field notes, and transparent itineraries. For travelers seeking vetted, licensed Nepali guides and logistics agencies, use our Plan My Trip tool to get connected with verified local partners.

How long does it take to get a response?

We typically reply within 24 to 48 hours on business days (Sunday through Friday, Nepal Time).

Can I submit an article or field dispatch to Wanders Nepal?

Yes. We accept first-person route dispatches and high-alpine photo essays that emphasize low-impact, slow travel and accurate logistical specs. Reach out via editor@wandersnepal.com with a brief pitch.
